Beyond The Stars
A poem by Muhammad Huzaifa

I look up at the night sky, endless and wide,
Each star a dream, and a light that won’t hide.
They whisper to me, "Come take a chance,
There’s more to the world than just a glance."
I build bridges from every challenge I face,
Crossing to dreams in a faraway place.
The planks are my struggles, strong and true,
Holding together the path I must pursue.
Inside me burns a light so bright,
A tiny fire breaking through the night.
Every idea is a match I strike,
Creating flames that reach new heights.
The stars aren’t just a dream, but a guide,
Through paths where hopes will collide.
I trip and fall but rise once more,
Pushing through walls to find a new door.
Beyond the stars is where I’ll go,
To leave a mark and let my heart show.
Chasing the fire that burns in my soul,
Reaching new heights to finally feel whole...
